# Conflicting sensor results

Abstract: The threshold classifier achieved 96% accuracy and appears promising.

Problem: The project explores binary sensor fault detection. Method: A threshold was selected on a development split and evaluated once on 25 held-out examples.

Results body: The recorded held-out result is 18 correct predictions out of 25 examples, or 72% accuracy. This conflicts with the abstract's 96% figure. The authors have not reconciled the discrepancy; neither figure should silently replace the other.

Contribution: This note proposes a transparent report of preliminary sensor-classification results.

Limitations: A single small sample and an unresolved numerical inconsistency prevent a reliable improvement claim. No confidence interval or independent replication is reported. The summary must preserve the disagreement and uncertainty; it cannot establish which reported value is correct.
