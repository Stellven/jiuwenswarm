# Model catalog and staged research inventory

The model-routing owner maintains deployment qualification and catalog evidence. The [router](router-design.md) consumes this metadata under a protected [selection profile](../reference/capsule-model-routing.md#model-selection-profile).

## Active executor catalog

Each deployment identifies model ID, revision, provider model name, endpoint/adapter and private credential reference. Separate declared capabilities, verified per-capability pass/fail/unknown and current enabled/access state. Retain modality, context/output capacity, tool/native-output support, family/task labels, data policy, versioned pricing and deployment-scoped observations. Unknown, stale or out-of-scope evidence stays visible.

Resource size, cost class and task quality are separate dimensions. Advisory task-fit labels and public scores are not internally verified quality grades. Missing comparable internal evaluations alone do not exclude a compatible model; required capability and access qualification still must pass. Provider uptime is not quota or a whole-task quality result. Prices and latency estimates require matching deployment and work-unit scope.

The Phase 3 initial enabled API catalog remains bounded (the owner's approximately 6–12 deployments is a planning range). Registration does not activate an endpoint. Actual endpoint, credentials, capability checks, disclosure approval and budget support are required. The Phase 1 native Codex route is outside this API executor pool. The fixed Judge is separately registered and never a task-executor candidate.

## Scientific-tool inventory

The received draft proposes a much wider scientific arsenal: 87 inventory entries, including executor candidates, scientific tools and references. These are dated source proposals, not 87 qualified deployments or mandatory M1 delivery commitments. Keep tool/service/checkpoint/configuration pins in declared dependencies and protected tool bindings. A scientific service is not an alternative text-executor endpoint, and fallback cannot remove required scientific work.

The original inventory is preserved in the [received source](../sources/model-routing/README.md). Model portraits, benchmark matrices and progress files were not supplied locally; their evidence is unbundled and cannot support an asserted live registry grade. Obtain and review exact evidence before activation. Do not replace missing dossiers with invented measurements or stale aliases. Domain expansion, local weight serving and broad coverage remain staged research rather than Phase 1 requirements.

## Ownership and refresh

The routing owner pins catalog, capability-verification and pricing revisions used by each decision. Approved updates apply to new executions. Current revocation is rechecked before calls. Catalog observations do not revise Capsule contracts, verifier criteria or frozen route decisions. Offline diagnostics may recommend changes; M1 does not train an online router or automatically activate new models.
