# Readable artifacts and evidence

Use descriptive JSON/Markdown filenames for Intent, Brief, research outputs, checks/verdicts/decisions and manifests. UI/Markdown views derive from the same immutable record; no independent summary truth. Humans must understand IR/decision without opaque `.bin` files. Raw executable/binary traces remain separately accessible where authorized.

Show run/node/attempt, candidate versus accepted status, criterion reasons, uncertainty, limits and exact source/evidence links. Read-only saved-run inspection never executes work or changes acceptance. In-progress exports mark missing outputs; redaction/changed content gets new identity and omissions/audience metadata. Export paths are confined, named and versioned; clients retrieve through authorization, never direct private storage.

Account/profile deletion differs from run/workspace deletion. Authorized local inspect/remove preserves honest missing evidence rather than reconstructing a false pass. Hidden fixtures/credentials never enter general exports or model context. [Manifest fields](reference/other-contracts.md#manifest-and-runtime-records) define identities and contents.
