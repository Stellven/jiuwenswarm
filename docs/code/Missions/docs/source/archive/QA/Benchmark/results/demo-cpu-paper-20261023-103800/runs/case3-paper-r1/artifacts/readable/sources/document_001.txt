Chunkers.Wecomparethreestatelesschunkers,eachusing
only the current version and fixed parameters, on the same
canonical text, in which each paragraph, heading, and list
item is one whitespace-normalised block and blocks are sep-
arated by a blank line. Fixed-size chunking cuts greedy, non-
overlapping windows of at most 304 tokens (special tokens
excluded) at token start offsets. Recursive chunking keeps
a span within its 400-token target and otherwise splits it,
in order, at blank lines, line breaks, and Latin or full-width
sentence terminators followed by whitespace, falling back to
fixed-size windows; the units are then packed greedily while
they fit the target. The rules are language-independent, but
Chinese terminators are rarely followed by whitespace, so
oversized Chinese blocks usually fall back to windows; such
blocks are rare, and in a fixed 24-article development subset
555 of 566 recursive chunks end at a block boundary. CDC

Recompute fraction and evidence recall are averaged first
within each article, then across articles in each language.

Verbatim presence. On the 17 common-success primary
articles in the 24-article development subset (Sec. IV), we
checked whether the complete text of every distinct recom-

Thisisthecasefor50%offixed-sizerecomputedinputtokens

CDC, and for 12% and 37% under recursive chunking. The
complete text of these inputs is present in the old version,
but not as a reusable input under the old partition. This is
consistent with boundary drift contributing to recomputation.
The remaining inputs have no complete verbatim match in
the old version; the check does not determine how much of
theirtextwouldrequirerecomputationunderanotherpartition.
Because the check does not establish alignment, it supports a
boundary-drift interpretation but does not quantify its causal
contribution.
CDCfailures.CDCfingerprintswhitespace-delimitedunits,
so a long run of Chinese text without whitespace is a single
unit; four articles contained units of 403–550 tokenizer units,
abovethe352maximum,andthechunkerrejectedtheseinputs
rather than truncating them. This is an operational property of
CDC: unsegmented Chinese needs word segmentation or a
fallback splitter, which would change its boundaries and was
not evaluated. Its cut points must also satisfy minimum and
maximum lengths, so after an edit CDC can drift for several
chunks before a valid fingerprint boundary is found.
