# Expert Weight Streaming Builder 1.0.0

`ranges(inputs)` validates the fixture schema against the exact upstream source seam. It preserves authoritative selected IDs, including repeats, while deduplicating byte reads. Bounds use original GGUF shard/data offsets, contiguous expert stride and original packed formats: F32, IQ1_M, IQ2_XXS, IQ2_XS. Packed block scale/metadata bytes travel unchanged. These declared shard digests must be checked against independently admitted assets; the planner does not authenticate model files. Model loader registration obtains fd/offset directly from resolved GGUF metadata, not these declarations.

`integration(inputs)` returns the source pin and material contract. In a clean owned checkout at that pin, apply integration_patch; place streaming_source at ggml/src/ggml-cpu/expert-streaming.cpp and streaming_api at ggml/include/ggml-cpu-expert.h. Obtain residency_header from the separately sealed engine.expert_weight_residency_builder release and place it at ggml/src/ggml-cpu/expert-residency.hpp. Place both CPU fixtures and check-cpu.sh in tests/demo-expert-weights. Record both sealed releases and actual material hashes in source provenance. The patch binds acquisition after authoritative routing and holds each thread's generation lease through the final ordinary CPU/tiled reader. The compact tiled tensor is a local view; selected IDs, row map, mixture weights and kernel arithmetic remain unchanged.

Activation is startup-only: both LLAMA_EXPERT_RESIDENCY=1 and LLAMA_EXPERT_STREAMING=1, plus every effective_environment field returned by the admitted residency plan. Require --load-mode mmap --lazy-mode on, explicit expert override 'blk\.[0-9]+\.ffn_(gate|up|down)_exps\.weight=CPU', no mlock/direct I/O, no draft model. CPU_REPACK/CUDA experts are rejected. The runtime validates the warm set against loaded tensors; invalid/mixed switches, unsupported layout or impossible budget fail. Oversized, busy or fully leased slots bypass to the original mapping and increment bypass. Short/failed reads or cancellation fail acquisition; the graph aborts rather than returning successful output. No prefetch or Engram interception is installed.

Rollback: collect artifacts, stop and join the owned process/readers, destroy its context, then restart the same candidate with both switches 0 or unset and otherwise identical arguments/assets. There is no hot toggle, cache resize or shared-service restart. Default-off does not create a registry. Keep stock A and same-candidate off A-prime as separate controls.

Counters: successful fills, cache hits, joined fills, explicit bypasses, failed fills, current/peak owned bytes, outstanding I/O bytes and active leases. They describe application reads, NOT physical disk traffic or critical-path waits. A timing profile and phase-tagged route capture remain prerequisites for performance claims or CC 3. Final log is a teardown snapshot, not proof of per-request engagement. CPU fixtures prove byte fidelity and exact routed outputs on the ARM ordinary vec-dot reader, including slot-pressure fallback while a lease remains held. Upstream disables tiled eligibility on ARM even with the force flag. The local compact tiled view is source-only and not execution-verified here; AVX-host validation is required before claiming tiled support. No whole-model or greedy-token acceptance is inferred.
