## Spark map and lazy-read admission

Require effective mmap and preserve the stock auto or on lazy-read setting in both the planned and canonical CPU fallback paths. At the pinned DeepSeek fixture, per_layer_token_embd.weight is 110,595,290,400 bytes and the demonstrated capped stock load leaves it lazy. Turning lazy reads off is not an admissible DeepSeek profile. Check that the expert projections remain ordinary mapped, byte-identical tensors and that the same lazy mode is held in all stock controls. Admission and failure probes must independently show the stock CPU complement and Engram lazy behavior.
