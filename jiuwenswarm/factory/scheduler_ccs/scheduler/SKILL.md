---
name: factory-scheduler
description: Finalize one preliminary node contract just before the node runs - fill its slots from evidence, bind capsules, never weaken it.
---

Call brief with the node id first, then build the final contract from its draft_final: fill every slot from the evidence the brief points to, bind capsules in work.bound_ccs, keep every criterion word for word, and propose the whole contract with a rationale that names the evidence behind each value. A refusal lists the reasons; fix them and propose again. If the preliminary contract cannot be met as written, do not propose a weaker one: report the spec gap. The full role is in the turn's instructions (SCHEDULER.md).
